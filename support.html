Frequently asked questions

How do I steer?

The joystick on the left moves the sampan, or walks you once you are ashore. On a keyboard, the arrow keys or W, A, S and D do the same. Drag one finger on the water to orbit the camera. Pinch, or use two fingers, to zoom.

How do I fish?

Cast the net and wait for fish to swim in. Or put a line down, strike when you get a bite, and hold the reel. If the tension climbs too high, ease off or the nylon parts. Squid and cuttlefish are easier after dark with the carbide lamp lit.

How do I sell a catch?

Dock at a harbour. The view becomes a walkable town. Find the fish market, walk up to the counter and press A. The grocer sells rice, kerosene, carbide and ice. The trading company deals in goods. The boatyard repairs the hull and sells upgrades.

What is Signal No. 8?

A typhoon warning. The radio and the temple fortune can hint at tomorrow’s weather. Staying out in a storm in open water damages the hull. Head for a shelter.

What happens at dawn?

The family needs rice and kerosene. Ice keeps fish that sat overnight. You can rest aboard until dawn from your own boat: dinner, the radio, mending, a story, then sleep.

Do I need to save?

No. The game saves on its own. Progress stays on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets it, and we are not able to recover it.

Can I change the language?

Yes. English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Cantonese and Vietnamese. Pick one on the title screen, or from the language button while you play.

Is there anything to buy?

No. The game has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Cash in the harbour is fictional.

The picture will not start, or it feels slow.

The harbour needs WebGL. Close other apps, restart the game, and open it in a current version of iOS. Battery saver, in the picture control, turns the scene down for older devices. If the shelter still will not appear, email us with your device model.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, in portrait and landscape. The desktop version is coming soon and is not available to download yet.
