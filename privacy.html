Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Tanka Tides 1962 is a fishing voyage you play on your own device. This page explains what the game stores and what it does not.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to play.
  • The game does not collect personal information, and it does not send your play data to us or anyone else.
  • The game does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • If you buy the Full Story, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or Apple Account details.

What stays on your device

To keep your place between sessions, the game saves a small amount of data in your device’s local storage: the voyage in progress, the fish you have seen, and settings such as language, sound and picture quality. This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes it, and the voyage starts over.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, or device identifiers. The game has no subscriptions.

The Full Story purchase

The Full Story is an optional in-app purchase made through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The game only asks the App Store whether the Full Story has been bought on your Apple Account, so it can unlock the story. It does not receive your name, email, payment details or purchase history, and nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party analytics or advertising tools, and it runs without an internet connection. If you download it from the App Store, Apple handles the download under its own privacy policy. If you visit this website, your host may keep standard server logs such as your IP address and the pages you opened.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking, no chat or social features, and no links that take children out of the game. The one optional purchase, the Full Story, goes through Apple’s App Store, where parents can require approval with Ask to Buy or turn purchases off in Screen Time.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about this game, email the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes what it collects, we will update this page and the date above before the change goes live.
